My Personal Values

Provider

- I am a provider to my family and to my coworkers

- I provide first, take last

- I work for those I love, not myself

Protector

- I protect those I love with my life

- I spare no expense to protect loved ones and the things I have built

- I accept my responsibility of those I am steward over

Honesty

- I must always tell the truth

- I must never lie for my own personal gain

- Honesty is more important than being right

Humility

- I accept when I am wrong

- I must put pride out of the way

- I must be pragmatic, not focused on personal gain
